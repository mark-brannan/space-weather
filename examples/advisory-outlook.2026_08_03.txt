SPACE WEATHER ADVISORY OUTLOOK #26-30
2026 August 2 at 10:24 p.m. MDT (2026 August 3 0424 UTC)

**** SPACE WEATHER OUTLOOK ****

Summary For July 27-August 2

An isolated G2 (Moderate) geomagnetic storming synoptic period was observed on 02 Aug (1500-1800 UTC). 

Two R1 (Minor) radio blackouts were observed during the week at 27.0243 UTC and 30/1700 UTC.

Two S1 (Minor) solar radiation events were observed with peaks at 30/2055 UTC and 31/1600 UTC.


Outlook For August 3-9

G1 (Minor) storms are expected on 03 Aug  (due to a CME persistent and waning disturbances) and likely on 19 Aug (due to a recurrent high speed stream). 

There is a chance for R1-R2 (Minor-Moderate) radio blackouts on 03-09 Aug due to the flaring potential of the current and returning regions. 

No NOAA solar radiation events are expected on 03-09 Aug.
